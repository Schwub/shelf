Copy a friend's tags, v2

Revision of the built copy screen (2026-11-27, after v1.0.32). Two changes: sub-tags show under their parent tag with a connector; a sub-tag whose parent isn't on the album still shows, with its parent greyed above it, and ticking it brings the parent along (grey tick). The target picker no longer lists every tag: likely targets first (the current one, same-name tags anywhere, a new tag in the suggested group, a new group), then all my groups folded; a group opens in place (one at a time), sub-groups open under their tag, and a search finds a tag or group by name. A sub-tag's picker starts under its parent's target. Everything else stays: nothing ticked, Tick the N that match, remembered groups per friend, Skip and the link on the entry.
