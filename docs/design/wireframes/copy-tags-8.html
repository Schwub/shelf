A friend's tags: tap a part of To to change it (v8)

v8 (2026-11-27): fewer steps than v7. The list: when her path is the same in my tags, one bar with '= mine' and Add. Where it differs or parts are new, FROM and TO, with new parts dashed and +, and Copy. To change where something goes, tap that part of TO. A sheet opens for just that part, with both bars pinned and the part marked: a tag part can stay new like hers, become one of my tags, or move to another group; a group part can stay, be flattened away, join one of my groups or move, and can bring its other tags along. TO updates at once; the tapped part is the start of the mapping, so there is no 'pick the start' step and no Change button. Check appears only when more than this one tag is affected. Mappings are remembered per friend as before.
