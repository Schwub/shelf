A friend's tags: From and To path bars (v7)

v7 (2026-11-27), after testing v1.0.37: show, don't explain. Every tag is a pair of path bars: FROM (her full path) and TO (the full path in my tags), each part a chip; parts that would be created are dashed with a +. No help sentences. The list has one main button per line (Add when everything exists, Copy when parts are new) and Change. Change is a guided three-step flow with the two bars pinned at the top: 1 tap a part of FROM to pick the start, 2 browse my tags and watch TO follow each tap (on one of my tags: use it instead, or put the rest under it), 3 check the result as From/To, with the other tags that come along expandable into their own rows. 'Use my Metal' and 'Forget' become choices inside Change (step 2 opens at the suggestion; a reset to her structure is the first entry).
