Copy a friend's tags, v3: Copy or Map

v3, after the user's model (2026-11-27). Each of the friend's tags on the album is shown in the friend's whole tree (group › tag › sub-group › sub-tag), not as a flat list. Every group and tag has two ways in: Copy keeps the friend's structure: parts you already have (same built-in key or name, along the same path) are reused, the rest is created, a group you don't have becomes a new group at your root. Map puts it anywhere in your tree: into one of your groups (tags matched by name), onto one of your tags, under one of your tags as a sub-group, or at your root. Mapping a group or a parent tag takes everything under it along; anything below can still be mapped on its own. Nothing is ticked at first; Tick the N you have; a ticked sub-tag brings its path (grey). A summary line says what will be created. A group's mapping is remembered per friend; a tag's is for this album.
