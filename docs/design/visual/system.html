shelf visual system · evening and daylight

Modern and flat with a few 80s cues: teak shelf lines, divider tabs standing on a teak line, wide Archivo type, a small amber indicator light on selected keys, and a five-segment rating meter (half a segment for half a star). One accent (amber), one control (the 44 px key, 4 px corners). Tokens and rules: docs/design/visual-system.md.
