Cassette key: four variations

All on the refined cassette key (V3); one idea pushed in each. Left evening, right daylight.

1 · Deep travel

More mechanical movement: a 6 px lip and a 5 px press.

2 · Brushed metal

A richer material: a fine, barely visible brushed texture on every key face.

3 · Engraved label, lit window

Finer details: labels pressed into the key, and the light sits in a small recessed window that glows softly when on.

4 · Pivot

A different movement: the key is hinged at the back and tilts when pressed, with a softly bevelled top edge.
