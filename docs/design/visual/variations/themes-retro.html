Retro colour themes: beyond brown

Eight theme candidates away from brown, 2026-11-25, design only: guitar finishes (sunburst, sonic blue, surf green, candy red, shell pink) and retro orange, plus a mid-century teal. Colours are inspired by those classics; the names in the app would stay generic. None has light windows: a chosen key is pressed down, its face tinted and its label in the accent colour. Teak boards become chrome where wood would not fit.

1 · Sunburst

The classic three-tone guitar finish: a near-black body, cream like an aged pickguard, and an amber-to-cherry burst on the primary key and the meter. Chosen labels in amber.
