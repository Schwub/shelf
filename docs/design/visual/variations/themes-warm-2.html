More themes: warmer, and retro-futurism

Six more theme candidates, 2026-11-25, design only. None has light windows: a chosen key is pressed down, its face takes a faint tint of the accent and its label the accent colour. 1 and 2 are warmer takes on Tobacco & copper; 3 to 6 go towards the retro-futurism of the original Evening theme (a soft gradient on the primary key and meter, space-age and hi-fi colours), but calmer. The current default comes first, for comparison.
