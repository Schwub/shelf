# Visual design files

**Final** (what the app is built from):
- `screens/` – every M1 screen and state, evening and daylight
  (`shelf.html`, `entry.html`, `search.html`, `signin.html`, `tags.html`,
  `filters.html`, `import.html`).
- `variations/c4-final-sheet.html` – the key in close-up; option B is
  final.
- Rules and values: `../visual-system.md`.

**Exploration** (kept for reference, not current):
- `direction-*.html`, `system.html` – early directions A–B5 and the first
  locked layout (flat keys, divider tabs).
- `variations/` – colour variations V1–V5, key and pressed-state studies,
  tab bar studies (T1–T3, piano keys, S1–S5; S2 chosen), the top-down key.
- `variations/themes-warm.html` – five warmer, calmer theme candidates
  without light windows or the bright gradient (2026-11-25, for TASK-31),
  next to the current theme. 2 (Tobacco & copper) became the default.
- `variations/themes-warm-2.html` – six more: two warmer takes on Tobacco
  & copper (Ember, Cognac) and four in the retro-futurism direction of the
  original Evening (Space age, Tube amp, Cassette chrome, Atomic cream).
- `variations/themes-retro.html` – eight away from brown: guitar finishes
  (Sunburst, Sonic blue, Surf green, Candy red, Shell pink), retro orange
  (Tangerine night, Studio orange) and Teal & mustard. App names would stay
  generic.
- `screens/signin.html` predates sign-in by code: the built screens (TASK-6) add a code field to Check your email.
- `variations/cover-background.html` – TASK-42: the album entry with
  today's plain stage, A (the cover blurred and zoomed in behind it) and B
  (a gradient from the cover's prominent colours), in Tobacco, Evening and
  Daylight, plus an album without a cover.
- `variations/launcher-icon.html` – launcher icon candidates: A three
  covers on a board, B the key with its light, C a record on the shelf,
  D the wordmark's s; each full size, in Android's masks, at 48 px and on
  dark and light home screens.
